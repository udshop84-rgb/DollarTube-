Cookies Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

How and why DollarTube uses cookies, and how you can control them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how you use the site. We also use local storage and similar technologies.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Strictly necessary cookies

  • Keep you signed in and maintain your session
  • Remember your currency, theme, and wallet balance locally
  • Protect against fraud and abuse

These cannot be disabled, as the Service will not function without them.

2.2 Performance & analytics cookies

  • Measure page loads, errors, and general traffic patterns
  • Help us improve speed and fix bugs

2.3 Functional cookies

  • Remember your language and layout preferences
  • Store your recent activity for quicker access

2.4 Advertising cookies (Google AdSense and partners)

  • Display ads that may be more relevant to you
  • Limit the number of times you see the same ad
  • Measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns

3. Third-Party Cookies

  • Google AdSense — Google Cookies Policy
  • Google Analytics — anonymous site usage statistics
  • Survey providers (Pollfish, BitLabs, CPX Research) — validate survey completions
  • Payment providers (PayPal, Wise) — only when you initiate a payout

4. How to Control Cookies

4.1 In-app controls

When you first visit DollarTube you'll see a cookie consent banner. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can reopen the banner from the menu (☰ → Reset Cookie Preference).

4.2 Browser controls

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

4.3 Opt out of personalized advertising

  • Google Ads Settings: google.com/settings/ads
  • Digital Advertising Alliance: aboutads.info
  • Network Advertising Initiative: optout.networkadvertising.org

5. Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. There is currently no industry standard for how to respond.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy as our technology and practices change.
